(01) — Fall Campaign

Meet the up-and-coming models in Céline’s fall campaign

Cool as it’s always been, Céline set a very high bar for itself when, in January, it released a single portrait of Joan Didion wearing dark sunglasses and a black sweater as part of its spring campaign. How are you supposed to follow that? The French fashion house’s answer, it seems, is: don’t try.

Rather than wrestling even greater celebrity talent, Céline has tapped a handful of relatively unknown models to go under Juergen Teller’s lens — a major label playing fairy godmother to a new generation.
