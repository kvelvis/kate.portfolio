MEET THE UP-AND-COMING MODELS IN CÉLINE'S FALL CAMPAIGN

Cool as it's always been, Céline set a very high bar for itself when, in January, it released a single portrait of Joan Didion wearing dark sunglasses and a black sweater as part of its spring campaign. Joan Didion. How are you supposed to follow that? The French fashion house's answer, it seems, is: Don't try.
Rather than wrestling even greater celebrity talent for its fall campaign, Céline has tapped a handful of relatively unknown models to go under Juergen Teller's lens. Please acquaint yourselves with Karly Loyce , Agnes Nieske, Irina Djuranovic , Lisa Helene Kramer and Federikke Sofie Fable-Hansen. While we love us some Hadids, Jenners and Smalls, it's always nice to see a major label playing fairy godmother to a handful of up-and-coming models.

    Originally launched in 1964, the MOKE quickly gained fame as a stylish beach buggy, symbolizing freedom and joy in coastal cities around the world. It even earned a place in pop culture history when French actress Brigitte Bardot was seen driving one through Saint-Tropez.
