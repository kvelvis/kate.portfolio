    04 — Info

    Before
    the lights go down.

    When do doors open?

    Doors open at 5:30 p.m. Support acts start at 6:45 p.m., The Weeknd takes the stage at around 8:45 p.m.

    What is the Chrome Suite?

    Front-of-stage standing area with a private lounge, early entry, an exclusive tour poster and a dedicated bar.

    Is there an age limit?

    All ages are welcome. Under 14s must be with an adult (18+) in the same seating area.

    What can I bring?

    Small clear bags only (max 30 × 20 cm). No professional cameras, umbrellas or glass.

    Can I change or cancel my reservation?

    Tickets can be transferred to a friend up to 24 hours before the show. Refunds only if a show is cancelled.
